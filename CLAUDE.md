# Repository map

Static, no-build exhibit deployed by GitHub Pages from `main` (custom domain in `CNAME`). Do not add a bundler, package.json or framework. Three.js r160 is a global (`THREE`) from the cdnjs script tag in `index.html`; the app code is ES modules loaded by `<script type="module" src="js/main.js">`, so local testing needs HTTP (`node tools/serve.js`), not `file://`.

## 1. Framework and hosting
- No framework. `index.html` is markup only; `styles.css` is the whole stylesheet; `js/*.js` are native ES modules with relative imports.
- GitHub Pages serves `main` as-is. `CNAME` = whogetstobuildwithai.com. Cache-control is GitHub's default (max-age 600), so a hard refresh is needed to see a deploy straight away.

## 2. 3D rendering and the camera
- `js/scene.js` owns everything Three.js: renderer, scene, `camera` (PerspectiveCamera, fov 38) and the `world` group that rotates.
- Look: a hand-built tabletop model. Baseplate = blue-charcoal sphere (`plateMat`, #434957) with a 512² canvas stud texture as roughness + faint bump; land = one InstancedMesh of 12,000 flat hexagonal discs (`landDots`, positions from `LAND_MASK`, 2° cells) whose colour skins the data layers (`LAYERS`, `setLayer` → `applySkin`) swap with setColorAt on toggle only; background clear colour `#22252d`, a star field in four sizes, the largest four-point sparkles (`sparkTex`), with a milky-way band (six Points draw calls), plus `shooting`: a pool of three gradient-streak quads fired at random every 2.5–9 s by `stepSky` (called from the frame loop; off for reduced motion; `fireShootingStar` and `shooting` are exported for tests; the streak path is chosen in screen space so it lands in open sky beside the panel and clear of the globe), a faint warm rim shader. Lighting: a soft warm key directional (0.8) + a faint cool counter-light + ambient (0.42) + a bright hemisphere (sky #c9d2e2 / ground #7a7984), exposure 1.12, no shadows, no post-processing. Keep it soft: a single strong key leaves brick sides black and reads as ominous.
- Towers: discrete 2×2 bricks, one instance each (a city tower that lands on its country point or on another city tower is slid sideways by `nudgeBeside` so it stands beside it) (`BRICKS_PER_INDEX_POINT` = MAX_BRICKS/100), chamfered geometry from `chamferBox()` (44 triangles), `brickMat` MeshStandardMaterial roughness 0.45 with a 256² noise roughnessMap on the high tier. Two InstancedMeshes (`bricks`, `studs`) shared by countries, continents and six city slots (`towers`, `cityTowers`); `writeTower` applies seeded per-brick jitter (rotation ±0.6°, offset ±1.5%, cumulative lean ≤1°, 1-in-40 proud) and `paintTower` the ±3% L / ±2% S colour variance; `stepTowers` animates with `easePlastic` (4% overshoot). `contact` is an InstancedMesh of soft dark discs under towers (fake contact shadow; spare instances reserved for arc caps). Data centres: InstancedMesh `dcMesh` (chamfered 2×2 brick) plus `dcStuds` (4 studs each) with `dcMat` (emissive 0.3 from the instance colour). Arcs: `js/arcs.js` (`createArcs`) draws each route as a solid plastic tube: a Line2 from the vendored fat-line addons (`js/vendor/three-fatlines.js`, global-THREE build of three r160's examples/jsm/lines) in the operator colour at 3 px desktop / 4 px phone (`worldUnits:false`, resolution set in `resize()`), over a black outline Line2 and under a dashed seam whose `dashOffset` moves once per frame (frozen for reduced motion and on the low tier); end caps are spare `contact` instances tinted to the operator. Curve maths is pure in `js/arcMath.js` (`arcPoints`, tested by `tools/arcs.test.mjs`). Draw-in 400 ms via `instanceCount`, fade-out 180 ms, hover on a data-centre brick highlights its arc (`arcsCtl.highlight`, fine pointers). HTML labels in `#arcLabels` ride on the far end of each arc (operator · city · why, or the country name) with an italic caption at the start; hidden behind the horizon and under the panel/sheet. The thin off-white selection `ring` completes the scene.
- Quality tiers: `quality.tier` "high"/"low" chosen at boot in scene.js (phone-like viewport, ≤4 cores, or `?quality=`), and `js/main.js` drops to "low" if the first 60 frames average under 50 fps; `setQuality()` removes the roughness/bump maps and flat-shades the bricks. Picking code is untouched by any of this (jitter is baked into the instance matrices it reads).
- Tools: `tools/perf-profile.mjs` (4x CPU, 390×844, draw calls / triangles / textures per scenario), `tools/view-shots.mjs` (default / Europe / Africa at desktop and phone, `--quality=`), `tools/palette-check.mjs` (ΔE incl. deuteranopia). `tools/arc-shots.mjs` (ZA and US selected, desktop/phone), `tools/arc-contrast.mjs` (operator colours vs plate/discs/bricks). All need `?debug` (sets `window.__exhibit`).
- Camera: `view` (in `js/state.js`) holds yaw/pitch/dist/pan and their targets; `applyView()` writes them to the world quaternion and camera position each frame; `resize()` uses `camera.setViewOffset` to centre the globe beside the side panel or above the open bottom sheet; `flyTo(ref, dist, towerLen)` targets a tower; `zoomAt`, `panBy`, `resetView` are the other movers. The frame loop in `js/main.js` eases `view` towards its targets and auto-rotates after 3 s idle.
- Picking: `castAt(px, py)` ray-casts in the world-local frame against every drawn brick (globe occludes); `hitTest` adds a tolerance ring (nearest-wins); `endPointer` resolves taps at the touch-down point and calls `select` / `selectDC` / `focusCity`.

## 3. Data
- `data/countries.js` (62 records; field-by-field JSDoc at the top), `data/datacentres.js` (49, each with `sourceUrl` or `estimated:true`), `data/continents.js` (6), `data/sources.js` (the source table).
- Every numeric country field is a provenance object `{ value, source, year, estimated }`. `normalise()` in `js/model.js` unwraps them at load, so the app reads plain numbers and finds the provenance under `c.provenance[field]`; `isEstimated(c, field)` is the helper. Sourced: population, GDP per capita, internet penetration, electricity access (World Bank), data cost per GB (Cable.co.uk 2023), usage index (Anthropic Economic Index, a Claude-usage proxy, 56 of 62 countries). Derived: subscriptionShareOfIncome = (20×12)/gdpPerCapita×100. Estimated wholesale: activeUsers, providerShare, growthCurve, cities, capabilities, barriers, facts.
- Regenerate with `node tools/fetch-data.mjs` (or `--offline` to reuse `data/raw/`). Raw downloads and their terms: `data/raw/LICENSES.md`. The Cable.co.uk workbook is gitignored.
- `js/model.js` joins the data modules into `DATA` and derives `dcCount`, `access` (via `js/accessGap.js`), `servedBy` (in scene.js) and the continent aggregates `CONT`.
- `js/colors.js`: `PROVIDER_COLOR`, `OPERATOR_COLOR`, `CONTINENT_COLOR` and `CSS_TOKENS`, a mirror of the `:root` tokens in `styles.css` (styles.css is the source of truth).

## 4. Intro overlay and side panel
- Intro: `#intro` markup in `index.html` (styles under `#intro`, `.ibox`, `#placeholder` in `styles.css`). Beats: headline, an opening paragraph (`.lead.open`), `HERO_STAT` (constant in `js/main.js`; the two country names are links that open that country, followed by the inline "How this is measured" link `#heroSrc`), an "Explore, be curious" line (`.lead.outro`), a CTA "See {Country} →" that opens the detected country's card (fallback "See where you stand →" focuses search) and "or explore the globe".
- `js/intro.js` wires the overlay as an `async` module that depends only on `js/geo.js` (time zone → ISO2 table, then `navigator.language` region; `GEO_OVERRIDE_URL` is the hook for a future `/api/geo`) and `data/countryNames.js`, so it is interactive at ~0.7 s on Slow 4G + 4x CPU; it then starts the Three.js download (`threeReady`) and `js/main.js` awaits it before importing scene.js/panel.js. Actions chosen before the scene exists are queued (`intro.pending`) and run on the first rendered frame, which also adds `body.ready` (fades `#placeholder`, the static dot-grid globe) (there is no auto-dismiss: the overlay waits for a choice or Escape). `sessionStorage.introSeen` skips it on later loads in the session; `?intro=1` forces, `?intro=0` / `?nointro` skip. The source marker sets `?panel=methodology` and dispatches an `open-panel` event (methodology panel still to build). `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off auto-rotate and the intro animations.
- `tools/intro-perf.mjs` measures intro interactivity on the throttled profile; `tools/shots.mjs` takes desktop + phone screenshots.
- Controls: `#controls` has Continents, the layer segment, a Routes toggle (arcs on/off, `R`, persisted in localStorage) and sound. `#siteShare` (top right, fixed) shares the page or the selected country's deep link via navigator.share, else copies the link. The prompt counter is hidden by default (`body.noticker`, "Show prompt counter" toggles it). Legend provider entries (`li[data-prov]`) filter: the top 10 countries by that provider's share stay up, the rest drop to one brick, and the Usage list re-sorts (`setProviderFilter`). While a country or data centre is selected, unrelated towers drop to one brick (`keepUp`/`LOWERED_BRICKS` in scene.js) and unrelated data-centre bricks dim (`setDCFocus`).
- Panel: `<aside id="panel">` with tabs Usage / Spend / Access / Key (Key is phone-only) and the card view `#v-card`. `js/panel.js` fills the lists, renders `cardHTML(ref)` and the data-centre card, handles search, pins, the bottom-sheet drag on phones (`.strip` = tucked away, `syncSheet()` re-centres the globe) and the live counters (`tickCounters`). A country card shows a "Your country" strip (`.mine`) when it matches the detected country (`MY_ISO` via js/geo.js) and a "Share {Country}'s card" button; estimated values use the `.est` styling.

## 5. Head metadata
- `index.html` head: charset, viewport (no user zoom), mobile-web-app metas, `<title>` "Who gets to build with AI?", description (<155 chars), canonical, `og:*` and `twitter:*` tags pointing at `https://whogetstobuildwithai.com/og.png` (1200×630), favicons, `site.webmanifest`, `theme-color`.
- The stylesheet link in index.html carries a cache-busting `?v=` (bump it whenever styles.css changes: GitHub Pages serves with max-age=600, so visitors otherwise keep the old CSS under a fresh page). The share image URL carries one too (bump it whenever og.png changes so platforms refetch it). `og.png` is rendered from `tools/og-template.html` by `tools/render-og.mjs` (plain HTML, no WebGL): the same design as the intro modal, and the renderer lifts the `#introArt` SVG out of index.html so the two never drift. The hero stat is the `HERO_STAT` constant in the template; keep it in step with js/main.js. Re-run after any change to the modal copy or illustration.

## 6. Routing and URL state
- `js/urlState.js`: `?country={iso}`, `?tab=`, `?layer=` are read on load (`applyUrlState()` in `js/main.js`, after the first frame) and written with `history.replaceState` from `select()`, `showTab()` and `setLayer()`; defaults are removed. `?country` also skips the intro. Other inputs: `?intro=0|1`, `?nointro` (js/intro.js), `?panel=methodology` (source marker; panel not built yet). `keyCollapsed` (localStorage) and `introSeen` (sessionStorage) are the only persisted bits.
- Per-country deep links: `c/{iso}/index.html` (generated by `tools/build-country-pages.mjs`) carries country-specific title, description, `og:*`/`twitter:*` tags with `og:image` = `/c/{iso}/card.png`, a meta refresh and a JS redirect to `/?country={iso}`. `tools/render-cards.mjs` renders the PNGs through `tools/card-render.html` from `js/cardTemplate.js`, the same drawing code `js/shareCard.js` uses for the 1080×1080 in-app share (navigator.share with the file, else download + copy link + toast). Re-run both whenever the data changes.

## 7. Styling
- Tokens on `:root` in `styles.css`: `--bg #1e2129`, `--panel`, `--line`, `--fill`, `--text #f5f5f7`, `--muted`, `--accent` (monochrome; no yellow accent), `--ease`, `--panel-w 420px`, plus `--c-*` provider and `--o-*` operator colours, which mirror the ABS-plastic palette in `js/colors.js` (off-white #ece7dc for OpenAI, desaturated hues, warm grey for Local; weakest deuteranopia pair ΔE ≈ 12).
- Type: editorial pairing after every.to. Big titles (`#title h1`, intro `h2`, card `h2`) in Instrument Serif (Google Fonts, `--serif`, italic emphasis on one word); labels/kickers/bylines in small tracked uppercase system sans (`--sans`), dashed rules (`--rule`). The intro box is near-black (#0b0b0d), square-cornered (10px), a two-column grid: copy on the left (kicker, serif headline, sans body copy and stat line with bold yellow #f6cf6f numbers (the user wants the body sans, only the headline serif), dashed rules, cream sentence-case CTA, sources and credit byline), and on the right the flat tower illustration `#introArt` (inline SVG: blue #2b5cf6 dot grid, cream/coral/black bricks, yellow label tags; js/intro.js relabels the middle tower with the visitor's country). On phones the illustration is a banner above the copy. Everything else stays on the system sans stack. Panels are frosted glass (`backdrop-filter: var(--glass)`), 18px radii, 1px `--line` borders. Phone layout (`max-width:900px portrait` or `max-width:600px`) turns the panel into a bottom sheet.

## Testing
- `NODE_PATH=<dir with puppeteer> node tools/behaviour-check.js out.json [baseline.json]` snapshots the UI (intro, ZA card values, tabs, layers, resetView, `?nointro`) and diffs against a baseline. Use it before and after any refactor.
- `tools/tower-pick-test.js`, `tools/tower-tap-sweep.js` and `tools/mobile-audit.js` predate the module split and reach into page globals of the old single-file build; they need porting to a debug hook before they run again.
